Approved Scope

Axis Command Center

Consolidating scattered responsibilities into a single, intelligently prioritized daily action plan.

Devpost Learn: Build With AI — Basics | Proof of Concept Blueprint

🎯 The Unique Kernel

A multi-factor, explainable prioritization engine that scores tasks by deadlines, importance, urgency, duration, and calendar availability—grouping them into Do Now, Do Next, and Do Later with clear reasons and instant dialogue-driven rebalancing.

👤 Target User

Student Freelancers who juggle coursework, exams, client projects, and personal errands across disconnected platforms. They open Axis to replace morning overwhelm with immediate clarity.

✨ The Relief Beat

Opening one unified dashboard that tells them exactly what to tackle first, explains why, reserves focused calendar blocks, and shows what can safely wait until tomorrow.

System & Interaction Flow

From scattered multi-platform noise to an explainable, user-directed daily action plan.

60-Second Demo Walkthrough Plan

Click each phase to see the exact on-screen actions planned for the video submission.

Act 1: The Problem — Fragmented Responsibilities

What the viewer sees: The Mock Integrations view showing realistic unorganized feeds: an urgent client email asking for revisions by noon, a Canvas assignment due tomorrow morning, a scheduled afternoon seminar, and personal tasks.

Demo takeaway: Clearly demonstrates the pain of app-switching and decision fatigue for a student freelancer.

Act 2: The Core Loop — Transparent AI Organization

What the viewer sees: Navigating to the Axis Dashboard and clicking "Generate Daily Action Plan." The incoming chaos instantly organizes into Do Now, Do Next, and Do Later. Each card clearly displays its estimated time, deadline proximity, and a badge explaining why it received that priority.

Demo takeaway: Proves that Axis is not a dumb sort-by-date list, but an intelligent scheduling copilot.

Act 3: The Kernel — Conversational Override & Trade-offs

What the viewer sees: The user opens the AI Assistant and types/clicks: "I need to study for tomorrow's exam before touching the client revision." The AI responds by explaining the trade-off (risk of client delay), recalculates the schedule, and places exam study into "Do Now" with the user in full control.

Demo takeaway: Shows that Axis empowers the human decision-maker rather than imposing an inflexible black box.

Project Boundaries

In for Proof of Concept 5 Areas

  • Responsive Command Center: Clean, dark-mode dashboard with Daily Briefing and Timeline.
  • Mock Integrations Hub: Pre-seeded data feeds for Email, School Portal, Calendar, and Tasks with refresh/toggle controls.
  • Explainable Prioritization: Multi-factor scoring engine (deadlines, urgency, duration, availability).
  • Do Now / Next / Later: Dynamic categorizations with transparent rationale tags.
  • AI Dialogue & Override: Conversational assistant that explains decisions and rebalances the plan.

Later Iterations 3 Features

  • Real OAuth Connections: Gmail, Google Calendar, Canvas LMS, Microsoft 365 APIs.
  • Autonomous AI Delegation: Auto-drafting email replies, rescheduling conflicting calendar invites.
  • Cross-Platform Sync: Native mobile apps and cloud database persistence.

Explicitly Cut for Hackathon 3 Decisions

  • Live External APIs: Prevents auth friction, API quota issues, and network flakiness during testing.
  • Multi-User Collaboration: Single-user focus keeps the proof of concept tight and fast.
  • Complex Database Backend: Client-side / local storage eliminates deployment setup hurdles.
