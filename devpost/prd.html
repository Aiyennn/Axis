Schedule Overload & Capacity Triage

Axis balances total estimated workload duration against actual free calendar windows between scheduled commitments, surfacing transparent deficit warnings when time is oversubscribed.

Acceptance Criteria
Briefing calculates total workload hours vs. open calendar hours dynamically upon task or calendar changes.
Overload alert triggers when required hours exceed available time, displaying exact deficit in hours.
Identifies at-risk hard deadlines and recommends specific flexible tasks to defer rather than silently truncating schedules.

Tri-Bucket Task Columns

Tasks are grouped into Do Now, Do Next, and Do Later columns with multi-factor scoring and instant direct card controls.

Acceptance Criteria
Cards display title, source integration tag, duration estimate, and proximity-based deadline badge.
Marking a task complete immediately strikes through the item and recalculates briefing capacity hours.
Clicking a card expands detailed AI reasoning detailing why the task sits at its current priority rank.
Manual priority dropdown allows shifting a card to another bucket with immediate schedule recalculation.

Interactive Daily Timeline

Continuous single-day visualizer mapping scheduled commitments (classes, meetings) against AI-allocated focus blocks.

Acceptance Criteria
Fixed calendar events are visually distinct from allocated task focus blocks.
Allocates Do Now tasks into the earliest feasible open calendar intervals.
Reordering tasks or completing work instantly adjusts the timeline visualization.

AI Assistant Dialogue & Approve Plan Changes

Ensures the AI assistant remains a transparent partner: major re-schedules surface explicit trade-offs and require user approval before applying changes.

Acceptance Criteria
Natural language challenges trigger an inline Approve Plan Changes card before board state changes.
Card highlights modified tasks and explicitly flags risks or deadline impacts.
Clicking Approve moves target tasks and updates timeline; clicking Keep Current preserves existing layout.

Mock Integrations & Rehearsal Controls

Pre-seeded feeds for email, school portals, calendars, and to-do lists, coupled with quick reset and mock creation controls.

Acceptance Criteria
Pre-populated with student-freelancer seed scenario ready for immediate 60-second video demo recording.
Reset Demo Data button with confirmation modal restores baseline state and clears test mutations.
Add Mock Task modal enables creating custom tasks with title, source, deadline, duration, and importance.
